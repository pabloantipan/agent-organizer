Deltagos

From deltos, the writing tablet, and strategos, the general.

Pablo's mark (deltagos-forja-intro.html) recoloured to the app's palette: the D in the accent, the big gear in the magenta tone, the small gear in periwinkle, on the app's ground, with Manrope for the wordmark. The form and the motion are unchanged: the same D, three travelling gaps with points and notches, speed varying along the D, and two meshing gears clipped to it.

Small sizes: 32 and 16 px

The whole mark does not survive at 16 px: the gaps are about 1 px and the gears' teeth under half a pixel. Each small size gets its own cut. At 32 px the D and the gears stay but nothing moves and there are no gaps; at 16 px only the D is left. The whole mark is drawn beside each, for comparison.

The gears' colours: three options for Pablo

The design system allows one accent, the magenta tone, and red for blocked alone; status hues are for status. Option A is the proposal: it uses only the palette's hues, and it bends one rule, since periwinkle is the next status hue used as decoration (the app icon's bars already do this). Option B bends nothing. Option C is the sketch's own ember and amber, which brings in two hues the app does not have.
