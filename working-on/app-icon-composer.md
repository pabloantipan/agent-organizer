## Spec
deltagos-mark shipped the mark as `iconfile.icns` (floating-icon Amendment 5).
macOS 26 draws any `.icns`-only app on its own light-grey plate: dm-build's
IconServices renders show it at 16 and 32 pt (Finder lists and sidebar), while
128 pt and up render clean (dm-ui's UI review U1, 5523e32;
`.wt-notes/dm-ui/f20-f21-iconservices.txt`). Aglaea
named this as a rabbit hole; Icon Composer's `.icon` (or an asset-catalog
app icon) is the format macOS 26 draws without a plate. Requires: Aglaea's
`.icon` export; how Wails v2.15 bundles it (an `Assets.car` via `actool`
and `CFBundleIconName`, keeping `iconfile.icns` for macOS 12-15).

Aglaea's export (Amendment 6, f9ee762): `docs/ux/inputs/deltagos-mark/app-icon/Deltagos.icon`,
generated by `export.sh`, verified on lodestar. The builder runs
`actool --app-icon Deltagos` into `Assets.car`, sets `CFBundleIconName=Deltagos`,
and keeps `CFBundleIconFile` on our `iconfile.icns` (with the 16 and 32 px cuts)
for macOS 12-15, never the icns actool generates. Never resave the `.icon` in
Icon Composer; regenerate with `export.sh`. Her rows F23 and F24 are A1 and A2.

## Notes
- 2026-10-09: added to sup50's task (window 8383) by the FSE.
- 2026-10-09 (aic-build): Wails v2.15's darwin packager writes only the binary, Info.plist and an icns encoded from `build/appicon.png` (`packageApplicationForDarwin`); nothing else goes in, so `make build` copies `build/darwin/Assets.car` after it. The car is committed because actool reads a `.icon` only from Xcode 26, which the macos-14 release runner lacks; `scripts/app-icon-car.sh` regenerates it after `export.sh`.
- 2026-10-09 (aic-build), for the designer: the car also holds actool's flattened legacy renditions named `Deltagos`, and a same-name appiconset cannot replace them (the .icon wins). An older macOS that reads `CFBundleIconName` may draw those and not `iconfile.icns`: no 16 px D-alone cut, and actool's glass edge (`.wt-notes/aic-build/car-640x320-A2-car-legacy-over-icns.png`). Unproven without a macOS 12-15 Mac.
