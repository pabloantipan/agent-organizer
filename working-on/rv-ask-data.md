## Notes
- Bindings for rv-ask-ui (`frontend/wailsjs/go/main/App`):
  - `ReviewAddressee(initiative, card): Promise<service.ReviewAddressee>`; `{seat: string, why: string}`, seat "" with why `no cell` or `no FSE or reconciler in the cell`; why otherwise `runs this card`, `the initiative's FSE`, `the reconciler`. Rejects for another machine's card.
  - `AskReview(initiative, card, head, symbol, state, note): Promise<service.ReviewAsk>`; `{marks: review.Marks, to: ReviewAddressee, thread: string, asked: string, error: string}`. Rejects (writes nothing) for a reviewed state, an empty note, nobody to ask, another machine's card, a bad symbol. Resolves with `error` set (the first error line; mark saved, no `asked`) when the post failed: that is FR-29's `Saved here; couldn't ask <seat>: <error>`.
  - `review.Marks` gains `thread?: string` (the review's thread, for Open the thread); `review.Mark` gains `asked?: string` (draw `? asked` / `✕ asked` when set).
- The ask's first line names the symbol by its map id. Choices and findings: `.wt-notes/ad-build/progress.md`.
