## Question

After two weeks of real use (milestone 2026-09-16), which of tray, fsnotify, launch-at-login or status drag is worth building?

## Options

- **tray**: the board at a glance without the window.
- **fsnotify**: rescan on card change instead of on demand.
- **launch-at-login**: the agents feed runs without remembering to open the app.
- **status drag**: move cards across columns, which writes a status to the file.
- **none**: the app is enough as it is.
