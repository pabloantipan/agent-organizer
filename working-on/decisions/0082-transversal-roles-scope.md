## Question

Pablo, 2026-09-30 (relayed by Hephaistos): "What I need now is seeing
Hefestos and other principal transversal roles in Deltagos App properly".
The FSE asked five scoping questions (thread 01M3SBV952KNT4XMBTXMQHZDFC).

## Ruling

pablo, 2026-10-04, in the FSE session on lodestar, answering the FSE's
questions:

- Placement: "Roles group in rail+Home" (their own group in the rail and on
  Home, above the initiatives).
- What each shows: "Live + context %, What it's doing now, Mail waiting for
  it, Initiatives it touched".
- Talos and Hermione (odyssey only): "Those belong to PLV infra, we don't
  need them here with skills, maybe naming it here". The FSE's reading, to
  confirm in the design: they appear by name only, marked as PLV infra, with
  no live status or skill on lodestar.
- Recognition and start: "Name; show only" (recognised by session name;
  Deltagos shows roles and does not start them).

## Consequences

Aglaea designs it (a proposed design spec); the FSE writes the technical
notes, the card and the accept record. Ariadna has no bitácora on lodestar
yet; the design says what shows when a role has none.
