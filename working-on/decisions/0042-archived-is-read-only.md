## Question

sup6's closing report on 0036's cards: FR-9 said an initiative that is not
active opens read-only. As built, only the rail folds it. An opened
archived initiative still allows Rule, card drag, comments, posting and
agent actions. And `queueOf` still counts inactive cells in the Agents pill
and in Conversations. The gate (G12) checked the rail only, so the card
passed. Build what FR-9 said, or amend it to what was built?

## Options

- **read-only and out of every count**: in an initiative that is not
  active, the app hides Rule, drag, comments, posting and agent actions,
  and says "archived: read-only" in the header. Its cells leave the Agents
  pill and Conversations counts. One small card.
- **rail only as built**: FR-9 is amended to drop "read-only"; an archived
  initiative stays fully usable once opened, and its mail still counts.

## Recommendation

The FSE's: read-only and out of every count. You archived seven initiatives
"so we focus in what we need"; a count or a Rule box from one of them works
against that.

## Ruling

Pablo, 2026-09-28, in the FSE's session (organizer-probe-fse): "do
recommendation".

## Consequences

On "read-only": the FSE cuts the card, with a gate that checks inside an
opened archived initiative and the two counts, for the next supervisor. On
"as built": the FSE amends FR-9.
