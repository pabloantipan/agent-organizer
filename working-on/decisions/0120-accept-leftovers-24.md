## Question

leftovers-22's reviews, answered by Aglaea (b131393). Three cards
(`docs/specs/leftovers-24.md`):

1. **Controls**: five things you click that Tab cannot reach today (an
   Agents group title, a chat's cell and person, a search result, a
   ruling) become real buttons or a link, looking the same.
2. **Rail keys**: each rail row is read whole ("organizer, personal, rank
   1, 2 now, 0 blocked, 3 next"); a group heading costs two Tab stops with
   its actions in a ⋯ menu; the collapsed rail and the Roles join the arrow
   keys, so the rail costs 3-5 Tab stops however many initiatives you have
   (Home drops from 54 stops to about 49).
3. **Drawer and skip link**: while a drawer is open the skip link goes
   away, so Tab never lands under it; the drawer's links get room for
   their focus ring.

No check drives your screen or keyboard.

biggest); basis: leftovers-15 to 22, this initiative (leftovers-22: 60 min).
