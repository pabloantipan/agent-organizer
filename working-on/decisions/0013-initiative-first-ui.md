## Question

The UI shows eight slices of the same data and answers none of: what is this initiative for, where are we, what is being decided, who is doing what now. Reorganize, or fix each tab in place?

## Options

- **initiative-first**: Home (portfolio + one Needs me inbox) and, per initiative, a header with goal, stage and the FSE's latest over Overview · Work · Roadmap · Decisions · Conversations · Agents. Calendar folds into Roadmap, Initiatives into Home, Settings to a gear.
- **keep the 8 tabs**: less churn; the slices stay slices.

## Recommendation

Claude's: initiative-first.

## Ruling

Pablo chose initiative-first, 2026-09-26, in the session that reviewed the UI.

## Consequences

Home and the initiative header are the redesign's spine; the mockups show them before any code.
