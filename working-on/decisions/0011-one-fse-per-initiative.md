## Question

How many FSE seats may an initiative have?

## Options

- **one**: one place where business intent and implementation are held together.
- **many**: split the view.

## Recommendation



## Ruling

Pablo, 2026-09-26: "Only 1 FSE allowed per initiative. Idea here is FSE could assist about what's going on along the whole initiative, joining business with how implementation is occurring, helping in building properly specs."

## Consequences

The `fse` skill's standing-up procedure checks for an existing seat first.
