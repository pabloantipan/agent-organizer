## Question

When Pablo rules a decision from the Needs me list, does the app write the ruling into the record, or open the file for him to edit?

## Options

- **write it**: a box on the row takes his words and the chosen option; the app sets `status: ruled`, `ruled`, `ruled_by`, `chosen` and the Ruling section, and commits. The first time the app writes a record.
- **open the file**: the app stays read-only over records.

## Recommendation

None; asked as open questions on the mockup page.

## Ruling

Pablo, 2026-09-26, answering the mockup page's questions: "write it".

## Consequences

The app gains one write path over records, for the owner's own ruling only, from the owner's own action. It never writes a recommendation or a proposal. CLAUDE.md's "the app never writes a record" changes to say so.
