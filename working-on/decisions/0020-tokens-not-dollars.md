## Question

What does the redesign show as a card's and a wave's cost?

## Options

- **dollars**: `cost_usd` from the statusline.
- **tokens**: input tokens and context fill per session, summed per card and wave.

## Recommendation

None; asked as open questions on the mockup page.

## Ruling

Pablo, 2026-09-26, answering the mockup page's questions: "just token consumption".

## Consequences

Work, the wave strip and runs show tokens (and context fill); dollars stay in `organizer runs` only.
