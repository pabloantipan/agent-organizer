## Question

In the wide class (0061), Home is capped at about 2,200 px: the list at
1,600 plus Needs me. On your 3440 ultrawide, about 1,200 px are left over.
Where does Home sit? (Aglaea's design, "Open, for Pablo", written into a
record by the FSE.)

## Options

- **left from the rail**: Home starts at the rail and the empty space is on
  the right. Resizing or snapping the window moves nothing.
- **centred**: Home is centred in the window, with space on both sides. It
  moves whenever the window changes width.

## Recommendation

Aglaea's, and the FSE's: left from the rail, so nothing moves when the
window is resized or snapped.

## Ruling

pablo, 2026-09-29, in the organizer on lodestar: do

## Consequences

The choice fills FR-4's placement in `docs/specs/responsive-home.md` before
its card launches.
