## Question

Pablo, 2026-10-05, in the FSE session on lodestar: "I've seen Teams feature
that reduces the full view to an small square along all desktop", then:
"Insetad of a minimum view, let's place an icon with an animation. It's
better behaviour. And on click the expand action, let's show the list of
initiatives, with a search, and then on click at one, we show that one at
full deltagos".

## Ruling

pablo, 2026-10-05, in the FSE session on lodestar, answering the FSE's
questions:

- Animation: "Decorative only".
- Where: "Every desktop only" (all Spaces, not over full-screen apps).
- How it compacts: "In team, when you're at a meeting you place window at a
  desktop; when you move to next one, it compacts automatically. Same. And,
  lets place a button icon for doing so at currect desktop where it's full".
- List rows: "Name + its signals".
- And: "Let's add that 'floating' impression Teams does to our app please".

## Consequences

The full window stays on the desktop where he placed it; on any other
desktop a floating, draggable, decoratively animated icon appears by itself;
on its own desktop a top-bar button compacts it. Clicking the icon brings
Deltagos to the current desktop as the initiative list (name and signals,
search); choosing one opens it full. The FSE raises a spike (0089) to prove
the macOS part before Aglaea's design is built on it.
