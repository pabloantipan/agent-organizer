## Question

The redesign landed on 2026-09-26: 11 cards, two waves, all passed review
(`docs/specs/redesign.md`). What does the organizer build next? Picking a
task is priority, and priority is Pablo's.

## Options

- **the roadmap first**: Pablo answers the intake (thread
  01M3FAWKV2159MCSH0AZZJWE08). The FSE writes `goal` and `measure` into
  `initiative.yaml` in his words, then proposes `working-on/roadmap.yaml` as a
  record (roadmapping skill). Costs Pablo about ten minutes. Without it the
  redesign's Home stepper, header and Overview read "no goal yet" and "no
  roadmap" for the organizer itself.
- **retire-keeps-the-cell now**: the one launchable card left (spec, gate
  and boundary already set, 2026-09-16), as a one-card task under sup4.
  Small, and it stops a full retire from erasing a cell.
- **both**: sup4 runs retire-keeps-the-cell while the intake and roadmap go
  through Pablo. They share no files.
- **pause for use**: nothing new for a week of real use of the redesign. Its
  findings become cards (the same rule as 0002's two weeks).

## Recommendation

The FSE's: both. The roadmap needs Pablo and not a supervisor, and the card
needs a supervisor and not Pablo, so neither waits on the other.

## Ruling

Pablo, 2026-09-26, in the blacksmith session, choosing "Both": a supervisor runs retire-keeps-the-cell while the intake and the roadmap go through Pablo.

## Consequences

The FSE spawns sup4 for the chosen card, or writes the goal and proposes the
roadmap once the intake is answered, or stops until use produces findings.
