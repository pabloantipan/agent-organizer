## Question

Pablo's three findings from the installed app (`app-review-with-pablo`): the
goal and measure fill half the screen; an expanded record on the Decisions
tab cannot be ruled; the page does not scroll. And the organizer's scope,
which stage 2's first exit item needs.

## Options

The FSE asked three questions in its session, each with a recommendation:
- **the header**: clamp goal and measure to two lines each with "more"; or
  show the goal only and move the measure to Overview; or shrink the header
  to one line once the page is scrolled.
- **where to rule**: also on an expanded record in the Decisions tab, with
  Needs me's Rule box; or only from Needs me, with a link.
- **the scope**: accept the FSE's draft, or write it himself.

The scroll was not asked: the body under the tabs scrolls while the header
stays, as any page must.

## Recommendation

The FSE's: clamp to 2 lines; rule also on the Decisions tab.

## Ruling

Pablo, 2026-09-28, in the FSE's session (organizer-probe-fse), answering the
three questions: "Clamp to 2 lines", "Also on Decisions tab", "Accept the
draft" (in: the lead's view of up to 20 initiatives; decisions and their
owners; agents, the mailbox and the Help. out: never writes cards; does not
replace Jira; business people do not log in yet).

## Consequences

- The scope is in `initiative.yaml`.
- The FSE amends the twenty-at-a-glance spec (FR-10 to FR-12), cuts two
  cards, and spawns their supervisor.
- `app-review-with-pablo` closes when those cards land.
