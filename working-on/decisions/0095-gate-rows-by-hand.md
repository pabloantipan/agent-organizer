## Question

Pablo, 2026-10-06, in the FSE session on lodestar, midway through
decisions-line-and-names' X1 trackpad swipe: "why am I doing this manually?"
That day he had done five by-hand takes for floating-icon-2, its installed
check (N3) and X1, all rows the FSE's gates gave him. X1 was checkable
without hands (no horizontal overflow, measured in the review build) or by
synthetic scroll events.

## Ruling

pablo, 2026-10-06, in the FSE session on lodestar: "ok" to the FSE's
proposal: X1 accepted on a measurement instead of his swipe (relayed to
sup45), and from the next task on a gate row names Pablo only when a seat
cannot check it, with the reason in the row. Feel and taste stay his;
measurable or simulable checks are the seats'.

## Consequences

The FSE writes every gate this way (spec-craft 5b's satisfiability check
gains "who performs it, and why not a seat"), and asks Hephaistos for a
shared tool that drives pointer and trackpad input against the review
build, so seats can run what today falls to Pablo.
