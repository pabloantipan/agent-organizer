## Question

leftovers-17's reviews, answered by Aglaea (237db96), plus two repeats from
the bitácora. Five cards, side by side (`docs/specs/leftovers-18.md`):

1. **One current mark**: the top bar marks exactly one place per screen. An
   initiative belongs to Initiatives (a quiet underline, the id as the
   title, no slash); Settings and Help mark their own icon; Needs me never
   looks current. With sync on, the bar still fits at 1024 by folding:
   memory short, the sync line into Sync's hover (`Sync · stale` past a day),
   the account to an icon.
2. **A fact once**: the header's Details stop repeating target and stage; an
   open card shows its goal once; hover raises a card only on its body; the
   header's pills wrap at 1024.
3. **Narrow views**: Overview stacks its columns and Help's long words break,
   so nothing runs past the right edge at 1024.
4. **Statusline**: some agents' memory/cost records stop after one sample
   (seen twice). Prove why first, then make the run's last line carry its
   last context and cost, or say "1 sample" instead of a blank.
5. **Build drift**: a plain `wails build` stops changing the generated
   files; one unused css rule goes.

No check drives your screen or needs the floating panel open.

2-5 cards each in 18-61 min with reviews, this initiative.
