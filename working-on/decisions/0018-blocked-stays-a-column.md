## Question

In the redesigned Work view, is Blocked its own column or a flag on cards in Now?

## Options

- **a column**: blocked work stays in its own place, as today.
- **a red edge**: fewer columns; blocked cards sort to the top of Now.

## Recommendation

None; asked as open questions on the mockup page.

## Ruling

Pablo, 2026-09-26, answering the mockup page's questions: "Should Blocked stay a column? yes".

## Consequences

Work keeps Next · Now · Blocked · In review (a lane, not a status) · Done.
