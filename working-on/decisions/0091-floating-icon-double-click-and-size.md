## Question

After using the floating icon (0088, 0090), Pablo asked for two changes.

## Ruling

pablo, 2026-10-05, in the FSE session on lodestar: "inmediatly, can we a
double click on the icon --> just expand app ?. Else, just add kinda two
buttons on the icon. Also, icon size for a laptop is correct, but for a big
screen, it tends to loose. Can we increment the size is those cases?"

## Consequences

Double-click is feasible (macOS click counts), so it is the chosen way; two
buttons stay the fallback. Aglaea gives the design calls (what "expand"
opens, how a single click waits, the sizes by screen); the FSE cuts a
follow-up card that launches as soon as floating-icon lands.
